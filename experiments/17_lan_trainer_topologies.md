# 17. Benchmark LAN Trainer Kit topologies

Use the trainer kit's own instructions and power off modules before changing
cables. Record each topology and the trainer's observed link/activity
indicators.

1. **Bus:** connect the stations to the trainer's shared bus and observe how
   all stations share the medium.
2. **Star:** connect each station to the central hub/switch module.
3. **Ring:** connect each station to the next, then connect the last station
   back to the first.
4. **Mesh:** connect each station to multiple other stations as supported by
   the trainer.
5. **Tree:** connect groups of stations through a hierarchy of central
   devices.
6. **Hybrid:** combine two supported patterns, such as a star connected to a
   bus.

For each setup, draw the links, count nodes and links, note the central or
shared components, and compare what happens when one link is disconnected.
Do not connect the trainer to a production network.
